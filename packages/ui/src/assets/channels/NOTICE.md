# Channel marks

Slack, GitHub and WhatsApp marks are copied from theSVG's public icon catalog:
https://thesvg.org/ and https://github.com/GLINCKER/thesvg/tree/main/public/icons

Source files: `slack/default.svg`, `github/default.svg`, `whatsapp/default.svg`.
Retrieved 2026-09-07. Files retain their original geometry and colors. Brand marks
identify the source channel and remain subject to their owners' trademark rights.
