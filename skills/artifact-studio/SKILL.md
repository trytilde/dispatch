# Produce a polished artifact

Clarify the audience, decision, medium, and delivery format from the request and available context.

1. Inspect existing source material and templates before drafting.
2. Establish a simple information hierarchy, then create the minimum structure needed to communicate clearly.
3. Keep claims traceable to provided or verified sources. Mark assumptions explicitly.
4. Match the requested voice and visual system; avoid decorative complexity that competes with the content.
5. Render or open the final artifact and inspect it for clipping, overflow, broken links, illegible contrast, and accidental placeholders.
6. Deliver the editable source and the requested export when both are useful.

Do not upload private source material or publish the artifact without explicit permission.
